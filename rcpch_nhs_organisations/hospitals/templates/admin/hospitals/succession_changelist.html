This table is the audit trail of mergers, acquisitions, splits, renames, and closures.

For forward events (a merger or acquisition happening now or in the future), prefer the wizards on the entity's change page:

  • Trusts: use Acquire another… on the Trust change page. (A full-merger wizard driven by ODS detection is planned.)
  • PDUs: use Acquire another… or Merge into a new… on the Paediatric Diabetes Unit change page. PDUs are not tracked by the ODS, so these are manual-entry wizards.

Create rows here directly only for historical backfill (events before the temporal layer was installed — see the Backfill merger… wizard on the Trust change page) or for ambiguous cases the wizards cannot handle (e.g. a split where children move to different successors). ODS succession semantics are occasionally ambiguous and a human should confirm the predecessor → successor mapping.
